# SDLC(Software Development Lifecycle) Models

| Model / Methodology | Type | Main Characteristic | Requirements | Testing | Change Handling | Delivery | Best Suited For |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Waterfall** | Sequential | Development follows predefined phases in a fixed order | Defined upfront | Mostly after development | Difficult and costly | Usually at the end | Stable requirements and regulated projects |
| **V-Model** | Sequential + verification | Each development phase has a corresponding testing phase | Defined upfront | Planned alongside development | Difficult and costly | Usually at the end | Projects requiring strong validation and traceability |
| **Agile** | Iterative / adaptive | Short iterations with continuous feedback | Evolve over time | Continuous | Easy to accommodate | Frequent increments | Projects with changing requirements |
| **Scrum** | Agile framework | Work organized into time-boxed Sprints | Refined continuously | Continuous within each Sprint | High | Increment every Sprint | Product development with evolving requirements |
| **Kanban** | Agile / flow-based | Continuous flow of work with WIP limits | Can change continuously | Continuous | High | Continuous | Support, maintenance, and teams needing flexible priorities |
| **XP (Extreme Programming)** | Agile methodology | Strong focus on engineering practices and frequent feedback | Evolve collaboratively | Continuous, heavily automated | Very high | Frequent | Software projects requiring high code quality and rapid feedback |
| **SAFe** | Scaled Agile framework | Applies Agile practices across multiple teams and organization levels | Evolve through planning and feedback | Continuous across teams | High | Frequent increments / releases | Large organizations with multiple Agile teams |
