# QA Concepts
## 1. QA vs QC vs Testing
Testing is a form of quality control. Quality Control(QC) is a corrective approach and oriented to a product that concentrates in activities that support attainment of appropriate levels of quality. Tests are one the main forms of quality control. Quality Assurance(QA) is a preventive approach and oriented to the process. It's main focus is the implementation and enhancement of the process. QA is applied to Development and Testing process. The tests are used in QC for correct defects, while in QA they supply feedbacks about the development process.
## 2. Verification & Validation
Verication and validation both works towards ensuring software quality.
Verification if the product was build accordingly to its design specifications while Validation serves the purpose of checking if the product right to satisfy real user needs.
**Are we building the product right? vs Are we building the right product?**

## 3.QA Mindset
QA mindset goes way beyong just testing a product. It's not about just the happy ending. It's about going that extra mile, thinking outside of the box, out of what was planned. Think like how a user will be using the product, how many ways they can use it, what impredictable ways they can use it?

Its not about pointing who did wrong, its about delivering the best product possible.
